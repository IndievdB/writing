    How it works

    The word finder searches 60,000 WordNet synonym groups and their definitions (a built-in reverse dictionary), then filters by sound using the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary — 125,000 words with phonemes and stress. The sentence box runs the same phonology through ~40 rule-based flow metrics: stress clashes and lapses, consonant pileups at word seams, alliteration and accidental rhyme, Latinate density, modifier pileups, clause shape, and more. The full catalog, with explanations, is in METRICS.md. Nothing leaves your browser.
