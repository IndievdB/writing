    What this measures

    Cadence runs your text through the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (125,000 words with phonemes and stress), a part-of-speech lexicon, word-frequency ranks, and human concreteness ratings — then applies ~40 rule-based metrics: stress clashes and lapses, consonant pileups at word seams, alliteration and accidental rhyme, Latinate density, modifier pileups, clause shape, and more. The full catalog, with explanations, is in METRICS.md. Nothing leaves your browser.
